# Naval Academy Science and Engineering Conference (NASEC)

- **Conference:** Sunday, November 1 through Tuesday, November 3, 2026, in Annapolis, Maryland. Participants arrive by 11 AM Sunday; the conference ends at 1 PM Tuesday.
- **Theme:** “The Science and Technology of Strategic Competition.”
- **Action:** To be considered, email WSE Advising by **noon Eastern on Thursday, September 17** with the subject “NASEC Application,” a brief paragraph explaining interest, and a résumé.
- **Selection:** WSE plans to notify applicants on September 18. Application and selection status are unknown.
- **Support:** WSE states it will cover transportation from campus, hotel, and local transportation for selected students.
- **Why it matters:** Multi-day engineering conference with technical panels, group research, and a final presentation. [Conference website](https://www.usna.edu/NASEC/index.php).
- **Planning:** If selected, the conference ends three days before the November 6 HDM/PWE/LADS triple deadline and one week before the November 10 LADS midterm; front-load that work.
