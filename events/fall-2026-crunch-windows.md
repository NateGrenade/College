## Recurring rhythm

## Standing trap — PWE attendance

PWE absences compound: from the third absence on, the final course grade
drops half a letter *each*, and three tardies equal an absence. Skipping a
Tuesday or Thursday to study for a LADS midterm costs more than it saves.
This is the one place where the obvious crunch-week move is the wrong one.
