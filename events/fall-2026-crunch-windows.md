# Fall 2026 — crunch windows

per-item view undersells the real problem. A 20% midterm three days after a
four-item pileup is worse than either in isolation, and `/weekly-review`'s
7-14 day horizon can cut a window in half and hide that.

Derived from INDEX.md due dates as of 2026-08-31; planning claims and
placeholder labels reviewed against INDEX.md and the PWE course schedule

## Recurring rhythm

Fridays are structurally loaded: LADS homework and HDM psets both default
there. **Sep 18, Sep 25, Oct 16, Dec 11** are double-pset days with no exam
attached. Wednesday and Thursday evenings are the standing work block.

## Standing trap — PWE attendance

PWE absences compound: from the third absence on, the final course grade
drops half a letter *each*, and three tardies equal an absence. Skipping a
Tuesday or Thursday to study for a LADS midterm costs more than it saves.
This is the one place where the obvious crunch-week move is the wrong one.
