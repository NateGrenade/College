Sums to 100%. Professionalism, Low Stakes, and Diagnostics are recurring
components rather than dated items, so they have no separate files.

- **Diagnostics** are short handwritten in-class writes, scored pass/fail
  on completion and good-faith effort, not polish.
- **Low Stakes** covers homework and in-class assignments.

## Deadlines and late work

- Unless noted otherwise, **everything is due via Canvas by 12:00pm** —
  noon, not midnight. Easy to get wrong.
- Low-stakes work: no credit at all after the deadline.
- Major assignments: 10% off per 24 hours, up to 48 hours; no credit after
  48 hours without prior arrangement.
- Extensions must be requested **before** the deadline, max 48 hours.
  Requests after the deadline are not considered.
- In-class activities and peer review cannot be made up without prior
  approval or documented absence; approved make-ups due within 48 hours.

## Attendance

- Two absences with no penalty, no documentation needed.
- **From the third absence on, the final course grade drops by half a
  letter per absence** (B becomes B-).
- More than six absences triggers a required meeting with instructor and
  academic advisor.
- Arriving more than 5 minutes late = tardy; **three tardies = one
  absence**. A single tardy of 40+ minutes = one absence outright.
- Leaving for part of class for another commitment counts as an absence.

## Technology

Distracting device use is treated as unprofessional: first instance is a
private Canvas comment, each subsequent instance cuts the professionalism
grade by half a letter. Some classes are explicitly tech-free with no
advance notice, so come prepared to work on paper.

## AI policy — read the original before relying on this

Partially captured; the left margin was clipped in the source export.
What is legible: AI tools are permitted for brainstorming topics you then
significantly develop yourself, sample outlines you develop using your own
reasoning, and style/concision/grammar refinement you apply with your own
editing. There is a bolded prohibition on submitting work substantially or
wholly generated by AI, and specifically on using it for **essays or major
assignments**. Any AI-generated material must be cited (APA "How to cite
ChatGPT" format) **and the original AI output must be submitted alongside
the work in its native document or transcript format**. Inability to
produce that original output may itself be treated as an integrity
violation. Undisclosed use results in a failing grade for the assignment.
