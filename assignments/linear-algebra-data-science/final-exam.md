# Final Exam

**Date:** 2026-12-16 (Wednesday), 2:00-5:00pm, in person, on paper
**Weight:** 39% of the final grade - the single largest component
**Cumulative.** Distinct from the LADE final (this one is LADS-only).

## Composition

Roughly 30% "core" linear algebra, 70% the data science material.

## Rules

- Three sides of cheat sheets, handwritten by you.
- No calculators.
- Three hours long but designed to take about two, so there is real time to
  check work - unlike the midterms.
- Everyone takes it, including graduating seniors. There is no senior option.
