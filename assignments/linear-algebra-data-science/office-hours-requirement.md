# Office Hours Requirement

**Deadline:** 2026-10-02 (inclusive)
**Weight:** 0.5% of the final grade

Attend office hours at least once - with either an instructor or a TA, in
person or over Zoom - and engage in a meaningful conversation. That is the
whole requirement, and it is free credit in a course where the curve makes
half a point non-trivial.

## Notes

- Office hour times are TBA and will be posted on Canvas. Between the
  instructor and the TAs there are roughly 25 combined hours per week
  (fewer once LADE and LADS split after lecture 27).
- Do not arrive having not started the problem set. The instructor is
  explicit that office hours are for after honest effort, and encourages
  conceptual questions, not just homework help.
- Same-day collision: HDM Exam 1, PWE White Paper rough draft, and LADS
  HW #5 are all due 2026-10-02. Go well before the deadline.
