**Date:** 2026-10-06 (Tuesday), 8:00-9:00pm, in person, on paper

**Shared with LADE** (EN.553.291) - same exam, same time, one large reserved
room for roughly 170 students across both courses.

## Rules

- One one-sided, letter-sized cheat sheet, handwritten by you. May be
  handwritten electronically (e.g. iPad) and printed. Keep it - Midterm 2
  allows you to reuse it plus one more sheet.
- No calculators.
- Recitation sections on this Tuesday become extra office hours instead;
  no attendance is taken, and you may attend any section or none.
- Exams are scanned by the TAs and graded on Gradescope. Regrade requests
  go through Gradescope with a few lines of written justification.
- No past exams are released, but most review problems are drawn from them.
