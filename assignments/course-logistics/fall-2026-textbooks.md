## Linear Algebra for Data Science (EN.553.295) — RESOLVED

**No textbook required.** The instructors distribute their own notes and
typed homework problems. Suggested references only:

1. Selinger, *Matrix Theory and Linear Algebra* — **free** at
   https://www.mathstat.dal.ca/~selinger/linear-algebra/ (printed ~$15-20).
   The one actually worth having; good extra problems.
2. Strang, *Linear Algebra and Learning from Data* — the instructor thinks
   the exposition is poor for a first pass. Skip for now.
3. Trefethen & Bau, *Numerical Linear Algebra* — advanced, good SVD chapter.

Resolved 2026-08-31 from the EN.553.295 syllabus. See
`assignments/linear-algebra-data-science/course-info.md`.
